About

IC-RATE 2026

The 2nd International Conference on Recent Advancements in Technology and Engineering (IC-RATE 2026) is an annual international conference organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, T. John Institute of Technology (TJIT), Bangalore, in association with the Department of Data Science and the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (IoT & CS). The conference will be held on November 4, 2026, in Bangalore, India, with a focus on emerging innovations in digital transformation, sustainable engineering, and strategic management.

Under the theme "Innovations in Digital Transformation, and Sustainable Engineering and Strategic Management" , IC-RATE 2026 provides a dynamic platform for students, researchers, academicians, innovators, technologists, industry professionals, and experts to present their latest research, exchange knowledge, and explore emerging technological developments. The conference brings together diverse perspectives from computer science, data science, artificial intelligence, Internet of Things, information technology, sustainable engineering, digital innovation, intelligent systems, and strategic management, fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and knowledge sharing.

The conference aims to encourage meaningful academic and industry collaboration, promote innovative research, and provide opportunities for participants to discuss emerging challenges and practical solutions. IC-RATE 2026 seeks to contribute towards a sustainable, digitally empowered, technologically innovative, and strategically driven future.
