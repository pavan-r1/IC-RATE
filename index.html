About

RATE 2026

The International Conference on Recent Advancements in Technology and Engineering (RATE 2026) is a distinguished annual event dedicated to showcasing technological advancements and innovations in computer science, engineering, and related fields. Hosted by T. John Institute of Technology (TJIT) on November 4, 2026, in Bangalore, India.

Under the theme "Innovations in Digital Transformation, and Sustainable Engineering and Strategic Management", the conference serves as a platform for students, researchers, innovators, technologists, and industry professionals. The event spans Artificial Intelligence, IoT, Cyber Security, Data Science, Cloud Computing, and Software Engineering. We invite academics, researchers, and industry experts to present their latest findings and forge future collaborations.
